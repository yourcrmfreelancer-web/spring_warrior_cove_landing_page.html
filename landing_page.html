A Letter From The Founder

Friend,

I'm a preacher's kid and an environmental consultant who sees the universe as an amazing freak show. For the last five years I've been restoring this 340-acre wildlife refuge because I believe life is better when nature, freedom, and common sense still have a place to breathe.

Here are the hard facts: about 46 developed acres, a mix of 1/2-acre to 5-acre lots, and roads and underground utilities breaking ground right now. Build a front-porch home, an RV port home, or a high-end RV base camp—then spend your time fishing, kayaking, hiking, golfing, biking, boating, and living outside.

And here's the culture filter: if you can't laugh at why Americans vote Republican or Democrat over a backyard barbecue, look somewhere else. We're building a place for neighborly tolerance, outdoor living, and people who still know how to disagree without becoming enemies.

See you at the Cove,
